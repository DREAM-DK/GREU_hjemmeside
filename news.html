News

What has changed in the model, the documentation and the projects around them. This is the GreenREFORM-EU feed, not DREAM's Danish news.

Latest

  • November 2026

    New Technical Support Instrument project: GreenREFORM-EU in 18 Member States

    The European Commission funds DREAM to bring GreenREFORM-EU to national teams in 18 Member States, from October 2026 to January 2029. The project, Economic Analysis for Climate Action 2, provides training, an automated Eurostat data pipeline and new sectoral sub-models.

  • October 2026

    Version 2.0 published as open source

    The model code is now available from the repository on GitHub, and the theoretical documentation as a PDF from this website. A user guide will follow. The release closes the KR Foundation project.

  • June 2026

    The energy technology model gets a new data module

    Technology catalogues can now be maintained outside the GAMS source, which makes it easier for a country team to bring in its own cost and capacity data.

  • 25 February 2026

    Final paper from the TSI 2024 project

    The paper presents the model together with two policy experiments: a carbon tax with revenue recycling, run with and without the technology block. It stays in Analyses after the news item ages.
