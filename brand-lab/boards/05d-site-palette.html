Why clay and not blue

A second cool colour would compete with the green and drift towards the EU flag. A warm earth tone reads as editorial marking, stays legible on paper, and cannot be mistaken for a brand colour.

Discipline

Green is never decoration. If something is green it is either the model itself or something you can click. That single rule is what keeps a five-colour palette from turning into a theme, and it survives contact with hundreds of pages of equations and tables.

Contrast on paper: ink 14.9:1, deep green 6.7:1, clay 5.5:1. All pass AA for body text, which the brighter Danish green would not have done.

What is inherited, and what is not

Carried over from DREAM
  • The disc as the model's mark, filled edge to edge
  • The lettering: thin, squared, white, tracked at 0.23em
  • The geometry: wordmark block at 74% of the diameter
  • Hind for everything else, open licence
  • The tone: flat, no shadows, generous space
Its own from here
  • The green, deep enough to separate at favicon size
  • The page palette: warm paper, green-black ink, one clay signal
  • The UI rule: green means the model, or a link
  • The layout, built for documentation rather than news
Still needs DREAM

The original model-logo vector or the typeface name, so the disc lettering matches exactly rather than approximately. Everything else in this sheet can be decided without them.
