Core idea

The model as a transition landscape.

Layered contour lines make propagation visible: carbon prices, mandates and investment travel through technology choices, production, employment, trade and public finances.

Why it fits

  • Centres the policy question rather than the software
  • Strong visual metaphor for scenarios and pathways
  • Bright, recognisable and optimistic without using leaves
  • Especially effective for reports, slides and public launches
