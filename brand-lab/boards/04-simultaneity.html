GreenREFORM-EU – Visual identity study
Direction 04 – open brief

Everything
at once.

Households, firms, fuels, grids and the public budget are not a sequence of steps. They are one system, and it resolves in a single solve.

Core idea

The model drawn as its own equation system.

Every behavioural relation and technology constraint is one cell. The mark shows the two blocks a modeller already recognises – the macroeconomic core and the energy technology model – and the cells where they are coupled. It is the picture you actually see when you open the system, not a metaphor invented for a brochure.

Why it fits

  • Structure at rest: no arrows, flows or growth curves
  • Reads as a scientific instrument rather than a campaign
  • One primitive, the cell, scales from favicon to conference wall
  • Says hard-linked before anyone reads a word
  • Ages well: version 3.0 simply lights up different cells
