Sheet 1 of 3 – the mark

Anatomy, measured off the Danish disc

Nothing here is eyeballed. The GrønREFORM logo was measured pixel by pixel and the proportions were carried straight across, so the two discs are siblings rather than lookalikes.

DiscFills the square edge to edge, exactly as DREAM's other model discs do
Wordmark block74% of the disc diameter, optically centred on both axes
Tracking0.23em on every line – the family's very open letter-spacing
Line sizesModel name at 1.0, qualifiers at 0.65, matching GRØN against REFORM
LeadingTight: 2.7 units between baseline and the next cap height
WeightLight, squared, monolinear, white only
The lettering here is set in Bahnschrift as a stand-in. Before anything is final, DREAM needs to supply the original model-logo vector or name the typeface, so the E, R and M match exactly rather than approximately.
The green is the model's own #10693A, not GrønREFORM's. Colour is where the family link deliberately stops: the disc and the lettering carry the heritage, the hue does the separating.

Where the EU goes

With no ornament, the name inside the disc is the only differentiator, so how it is set is now the real design decision.

A – EU as a tagKeeps the two-line family silhouette. But the eye reads GREEN, EU, REFORM, which is not the name.

B – three lines, EU closingShown aboveReads top to bottom in the right order and keeps the block roughly square.

C – three lines, flush leftThe most literal copy of the Danish stack. The block collapses to the left and looks unfinished.

D – rule, then the regionMost official in tone. Also the furthest from the family, because no other disc carries a rule.

Settled: its own green

Sharing GrønREFORM's green would have made the two models one green dot at favicon size, where the lettering is gone. The international model takes a deeper green instead. The heritage survives because it never lived in the hue: it lives in the disc, the geometry and the lettering.
