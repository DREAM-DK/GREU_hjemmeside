GreenREFORM-EU – Visual identity study
Direction 05 – DREAM heritage

The next disc
in the family.

DREAM already runs a model-logo system. The international model joins it instead of competing with it.

Core idea

Inherited, not invented.

Every DREAM model is a filled disc carrying a thin squared wordmark, and nothing else. GreenREFORM-EU inherits the disc, the geometry and the lettering, adds no ornament, and takes its own deep green – exactly as MAKRO, SMILE and REFORM each take their own colour. The heritage is in the form, not the hue.

The family, and the newcomer
DREAM · MAKRO · REFORM · SMILE · GrønREFORM
from DREAM's own model logo files, plus #10693A

Why it fits

  • Instantly legible to anyone who knows the Danish model
  • Inherits the form, so the site palette is free to be its own
  • Separates from GrønREFORM at any size, down to a favicon
  • Country teams are handled in type, not bolted onto the mark
  • Low risk, and no longer the least distinctive option
