# GreenREFORM-EU visual identity lab

Experimental work only. The implemented mockup is `mockups-graphic/`. Do not
treat the boards as live copy.

## The identity problem

The model is not simply green. Its distinctive feature is structural:
macroeconomy and energy technology are solved together. It is also a common,
modular architecture that countries calibrate to their own data.

The identity should therefore feel:

- analytical and credible enough for finance ministries;
- modern enough for an international model-development community;
- dynamic, without looking like a campaign organisation;
- environmental without relying on leaves, globes or energy icons;
- usable beyond version 2.0.

## Five directions

Directions 01-03 responded to the brief above. Directions 04 and 05 were added
later as the two poles of the real decision: invent something of our own, or
join what DREAM already has.

### 01  Coupled Fields

Two flow fields converge into one system.

This is the most direct visual expression of **hard-linking**. The mark remains
legible at small size, while the flow field can expand into a website hero,
report cover or presentation background.

**Current recommendation:** strongest strategic fit. It owns the distinctive
model proposition rather than a generic climate or network idea.

Palette:

- Midnight `#071A33`
- Policy blue `#2864DC`
- System teal `#28B7B0`
- Transition green `#72E18C`

### 02  Transition Surface

A policy shock propagates across a layered economic landscape.

This direction is the most public-facing and optimistic. It is especially
strong for policy reports, scenarios and launch presentations. It says more
about **what the model is used for** than how it is built.

Palette:

- Fiscal navy `#09284C`
- Depth blue `#075E7B`
- Technology teal `#00A69B`
- Signal lime `#B8EF35`

### 03  Modular Constellation

A common core connects modules, country calibrations and projects.

This is the most technical and extensible direction. It can grow into an icon
family for country teams and model modules. Its risk is that network/orbit
imagery is less specific to GreenREFORM-EU than the coupled-field idea.

Palette:

- Core indigo `#081B3A`
- EU blue `#4E9DFF`
- Module teal `#52D7C8`
- Signal orange `#FF9B52`

### 04 - Simultaneity (open brief)

Made with no constraints from the earlier discussion. The other four directions
are all pictures of *motion*: things flow, propagate or orbit. This one is a
picture of *structure at rest*.

The mark is the sparsity pattern of the model's own equation system. One block
is the macroeconomic core, one block is the energy technology model, and the
bright cells in between are the coupling. It is the diagram modellers actually
look at, rendered as a logo, and it states hard-linking without a caption.

The single primitive is the cell, so the system scales without redrawing: a
16 px favicon, a report cover, a conference backdrop, and eventually a version
3.0 that simply lights up different cells.

**Risk:** it is cold and it is unfamiliar. It will read as an instrument, which
is right for ministries and model developers, and wrong for a press release.

Palette:

- Ink `#0C1116`
- Ultramarine `#2D2BE0`
- Coupling mint `#19E3A7`
- Bone `#F1EEE6`

Type: Space Grotesk for display, IBM Plex Mono for every number, variable name
and file path. Both open licence. The boards render in a system fallback.

### 05 - DREAM heritage

Built directly on what dreamgruppen.dk already uses, not on an interpretation
of it.

DREAM runs a model-logo family: each model is a flat filled disc carrying a
thin, wide, squared wordmark in white, and nothing else. GreenREFORM-EU is
drawn as the next disc in that family. It inherits the disc, the geometry and
the lettering, adds no ornament, and takes its own colour, exactly as MAKRO,
SMILE and REFORM each take theirs. The heritage is in the form, not the hue.

An earlier version of this direction put a ring of twelve dots around the disc
to stand for the country calibrations. It was dropped: no other disc in the
family carries anything outside its edge, and the ring made the international
model look like a special case. Country work is handled in type instead.

A second earlier version reused GronREFORM's green unchanged. That was dropped
too. At favicon size the lettering disappears and the two models became the
same green dot, and carrying the disc and the typography turned out to be
enough heritage on its own. The international model now takes a deep green of
its own, which also frees the website from matching dreamgruppen.dk.

Three sheets elaborate the direction:

- `boards/05b-dream-heritage-system.png` - anatomy measured off the Danish
  disc, four options for where EU sits in the wordmark, behaviour at small
  sizes, and one-colour variants.
- `boards/05c-dream-heritage-applications.png` - subsite header, the disc
  standing in the model family, a publication cover, and country teams.
- `boards/05d-site-palette.png` - the independent site palette, the same five
  colours applied to a page, and a ledger of what is inherited and what is not.

One thing is still open, and it is not a drawing problem: **where EU goes**.
Reading order rules out putting it beside GREEN on the top line. Three lines
with EU closing bottom right is the working answer; sheet 05b shows four.

**Risk:** the lowest-risk direction of the five. It reads as a DREAM product
first, which may make GreenREFORM-EU harder to establish as an independent
European asset later.

Palette. The mark colour is the model's own; nothing here is sampled from
dreamgruppen.dk:

- Deep green `#10693A` - the mark, links, primary buttons, active states
- Ink `#1B2620` - all text, green-black rather than neutral
- Paper `#F7F7F4` - page background, warm so long pages do not glare
- Mist `#E7EBE6` - rules, table borders, code chips, quiet surfaces
- Clay `#A04A26` - the single signal colour: new, changed, caution

On paper the contrast ratios are ink 14.9:1, deep green 6.7:1 and clay 5.5:1,
all of which pass AA for body text. GronREFORM's brighter `#31B149` would not
have.

The working rule for the whole site: green is never decoration. If something is
green it is either the model itself or something you can click.

Type: Hind at weight 500. This is what the Danish site serves today and it is
open licence, so the family voice carries over even though the colours no
longer match. The disc lettering is approximated here with Bahnschrift; the
sheets in this folder still show that approximation.

The site itself no longer does. `mockups-graphic/assets/mark.svg` now carries
the outlines lifted straight out of DREAM's own
`Modellogo_GREEN_REFORM_100mm_RGB.svg`, so the disc is set in the same bespoke
alphabet as the DREAM wordmark and the other four model discs. One gap remains:
that alphabet has never been drawn with a U, so the U of EU is constructed from
its own metrics rather than copied. See the `<desc>` in `mark.svg`.

## Heritage evidence

Direction 05 is not a guess. These values were read off
`dreamgruppen.dk/modeller-og-metoder/groenreform` on 19 September 2026. Only
the typography and the disc geometry are carried over; the colours below are
recorded for reference and for keeping the new green clear of them.

| Element | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Body and headings | Hind, fallback Helvetica/Arial |
| Text colour | `#3D3D3D` |
| H1 weight | 500, no extra letter-spacing |
| Surfaces | `#FFFFFF`, `#F2F2F2`, green tint `#9DE4AA` |
| Model discs | DREAM `#DE0D0D`, MAKRO `#0F837D`, REFORM `#1F8FBB`, SMILE `#F76E00`, GronREFORM `#31B149` |

The disc values were first read off the rendered page, which put four of the
five a digit out. They have since been corrected against DREAM's own logo
files in `VisueltDesign/Logo/DREAM logoer_2021_01_11/`, which are the
authority. Only SMILE was right the first time.

The GronREFORM disc was also measured pixel by pixel, and direction 05 reuses
the result rather than approximating it:

| Geometry | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Disc | Fills its square edge to edge |
| Wordmark block | 74% of the disc diameter, optically centred on both axes |
| Tracking | 0.23em on every line |
| Size ratio | Qualifier line at 0.65 of the model-name line |
| Leading | 2.7 units from baseline to the next cap height, at a 184-unit disc |

The GronREFORM disc itself is saved in `reference/` for comparison. It is
DREAM's artwork, kept here only as a reference while designing, and it must not
be redistributed as part of the new site.

logos and should not be treated as scientifically meaningful diagrams.

## Typography

The boards suggest Inter, Manrope and IBM Plex Sans. These choices are not
licence-cleared or tested in GoBasic yet. Practical fallback is Aptos / Segoe UI.
Before adopting a font:

1. Confirm web-font support and licence with DREAM/Ber.
2. Test mathematical symbols, tables and tabular numerals.
3. Keep technical documentation typography separate if LaTeX requires it.

## Next decision

The first question is not which drawing is nicest. It is whether
GreenREFORM-EU is a DREAM product wearing DREAM's clothes (direction 05) or a
European asset with its own identity that DREAM happens to maintain
(directions 01-04). That is an internal DREAM discussion. These directions
exist to inform it, not to pre-empt it.

Once that is settled, choose on the **idea**, not small details of the current
logo. Refinement should then cover:

- mark geometry and optical balance;
- horizontal, stacked, symbol-only and monochrome variants;
- DREAM endorsement line (A model from DREAM);
- small-size and accessibility testing;
- website header, report cover and presentation templates;
- rules for versions, projects and country applications.

No direction should be put into the website until DREAM confirms whether the
GreenREFORM-EU identity may stand alone or must lock up with the DREAM logo.
