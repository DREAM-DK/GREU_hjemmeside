Projects

Projects that built GreenREFORM-EU, and projects that use it. The first three are generations of the model, all delivered by DREAM. The rest are adaptations of the model to specific needs in different countries, and DREAM works on one of them. A result from one of those projects belongs to the team that produced it. It is not a property of GreenREFORM-EU. Each project page lists the analyses that came out of it.

2024 – 2025 · European Commission

Technical Support Instrument — version 1.0

The pilot that created GreenREFORM-EU. One public analysis so far: the February 2026 paper.

2026 · KR Foundation

Version 2.0

A one-year continuation of TSI 2024. It refined the model, wrote the documentation, released the model as open source, and supported the teams in Austria, Belgium, Finland and Italy.

2026 – 2029 · European Commission

Technical Support Instrument — towards version 3.0

Brings GreenREFORM-EU to 18 Member States: training, a Eurostat data pipeline, an open-source Julia code base and new sectoral sub-models. Analyses will be listed here as they are published.

2026 – 2027 · Danish Ministry of Foreign Affairs

Viet Nam

Led by the University of Copenhagen with three Vietnamese policy institutes. GreenREFORM-VN, a Vietnamese version of the model with a distributional module, to assess the energy transition. DREAM collaborates on the modelling.

2026 – 2027 · TAH Foundation

FinReform

A Finnish project, coordinated by the Natural Resources Institute Finland. A Finnish version of GreenREFORM-EU, to assess the effects of climate policy on the economy and emissions.

2024 – 2027 · Energy Transition Fund

Belgium: CEEP-IT

A Belgian research project coordinated by VITO/EnergyVille, with the Federal Planning Bureau and ICEDD. GreenREFORM-BE, the Belgian version of GreenREFORM-EU, is linked with a detailed energy system model to study the future of energy-intensive industry.
