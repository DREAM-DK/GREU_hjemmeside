# Open questions for DREAM

These block a public site. They are not copy problems.

1. **Licence.** The site may say the model is open source. The documentation
   Rights section is still a placeholder. Confirm the licence text and the
   public wording. Until then name no licence and invent no licence page.
2. **Public PDF.** May the compiled documentation be offered for download? If
   yes, which edition and under what conditions? **Settled 10 November 2026:**
   `content/Documentation/greu_master_merged_v4_EN.pdf` was supplied for the
   site, and the Download button points at it.
3. **Site URL.** Canonical host and whether it sits under dreamgruppen.dk or
   on its own domain.
4. **Logo and visual identity.** Which DREAM / GreenREFORM-EU marks may be
   used, and in which colour.
5. **Contact.** Named inbox, form, or a link to dreamgruppen.dk only.
6. **Access terms.** The TSI 2024 results are limited to non-commercial,
   non-competitive use. Does the open-source licence for 2.0 carry the same
   limit, or replace it? What should the site say about it, and about how a
   ministry gets the Danish benchmark data?
7. **TSI country list.** Chapter 1 names Belgium, Finland, Austria, Italy and
   Poland as the 2024 pilot. The 2.0 proposal and March 2026 meeting name four
   remaining partners (AT, FI, IT, BE); one original institution withdrew.
   Confirm the public list.
8. **Module file names.** Documentation cites prefixed names
   (`03_production.gms`); the repository files are unprefixed
   (`production.gms`). Outstanding issue A1. The site should not send users to
   the prefixed names unless it also says they are documentation names.
9. **Repository.** The model is described as open source. Where is the
   repository, and when does it go public? Until DREAM gives the address, the
   site links to no repository and does not say the model has been released.
   **Settled 10 November 2026:** https://github.com/DREAM-DK/GREU (public).
   Linked from Get the model, News and Version 2.0. The repository has no
   tagged release yet, so the "Download release 2.0" button was removed. Put
   it back, pointing at the release page, when DREAM tags one.
10. **TSI 2026.** **Parked on 10 November 2026** until the contract is
    settled: the site does not mention the project. See
    `content/parked/tsi-2026.md`. The rest of this item applies on restoring.
    Settled on 3 November 2026: the grant was awarded, the site
    does not launch before the grant agreement is signed, and the project may
    be described as real. What remains is narrower. Does SG REFORM treat the
    18-country list and the two-track split as public, and does each country
    agree to be named? The grant requires the EU emblem and the Annex 5
    funding acknowledgement on the project website: get the exact wording and
    add it to the footer. Confirm "towards version 3.0", which the grant does
    not use.
11. **TIMES.** Belgium (and Italy) describe linking or catalogues from TIMES.
    Core-model pages stay hard-link only and name no external model. Still to
    confirm: whether the Belgium project page may name TIB3R, which its own
    deliverable does.
12. **Which master is live.** Working assumption, 5 November 2026:
    `greu_master_merged_v3_EN.tex`, drafted by Karl. The site's files have been
    pointed at it. **Still to confirm with Karl**, because this is an
    assumption rather than his word.

    Worth confirming rather than leaving. All three masters compile to 124
    pages, so page count does not distinguish them, and `v3` differs from `v1`
    in five of the seven chapter files (`chapter02_v6`, `chapter03_v5`,
    `chapter04_v6`, `chapter05_v4`, `chapter06_v4`). Chapter 4 is three
    revisions apart. Everything the site says about government and public
    finances rests on which of those is current. `AGENTS.md` in `FinalVersion`
    still names a different set again and is not ours to edit.

    **Update, 10 November 2026.** The published PDF is built from
    `greu_master_merged_v4_EN.tex`, which is not in `FinalVersion`. Its text
    differs from the `v3` PDF only by a new paragraph in Chapter 6.3 (no
    unemployment rate) and the date. Still to ask: where the `v4` `.tex` is,
    and which chapter files it names.
13. **Web accessibility.** Is DREAM a public body under the Danish web
    accessibility act? If yes, the site needs a statement produced in
    Digitaliseringsstyrelsen's WAS-Tool and a telephone contact, and the
    documentation PDFs must be tagged to EN 301 549 chapter 10. The pages
    themselves already meet WCAG 2.1 AA. See
    [../ACCESSIBILITY.md](../ACCESSIBILITY.md).
