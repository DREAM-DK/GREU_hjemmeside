## Analyses versus the rest

**Analyses** answer: has anyone scored a question like mine? When there are
enough of them, filter by policy question, not by grant. No filter at launch
(decided 10 November 2026; see `content/pages/applications.md`).

**Projects** answer: who funded the work, and who built the model as against who
uses it. The hub originally held only the generations of the model. It now also
holds projects by other institutions that adapt it, which is why the Projects
node names all six rather than a generation.

**News** announces a paper. The analysis record keeps it.

**Documentation** explains the model. It is not a library of policy runs.

Do not list internal country notes. Do not treat Danish GrønREFORM papers as
GreenREFORM-EU analyses.
