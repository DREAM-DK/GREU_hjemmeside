## Why it was set up

The TSI 2024 project ran for one year and ended in December 2025. Four of the
five institutions that took part were then building national data sets. A
break at that point risked losing the momentum, and with it the teams.

The KR Foundation funded a further year so that the work could continue without
a gap. The foundation had also supported the original Danish GreenREFORM
through the Green GDP project.

*Source: KR proposal, August 2025, sections 2 and 4; DREAM news item,
25 February 2026.*

## What it set out to do

- Strengthen the four institutions' capacity to analyse climate policy with the
  model, so that each has a model calibrated to its own data.
- Refine the model and generalise its architecture, so that it fits a broader
  range of countries.
- Publish a refined and well-documented version 2.0 as open source.
- Prepare the ground for further projects with other countries.

*Source: KR proposal, section 5; Chapter 1 § Background.*

## What the project did

- **Model development.** Refinements agreed with the four teams, the
  theoretical documentation, a user guide, and publication.
- **Data and implementation.** Individual support to each team: data structure
  and aggregation choices, calibration, error resolution, and reading the
  results.
- **Capacity building.** Online workshops and introductions for new staff,
  scheduled as the teams needed them.
- **Viet Nam.** A small part of the effort supported the University of
  Copenhagen's Viet Nam project. That project has its own funding and its own
  entry under Projects.

*Source: project plan, version 2, 16 February 2026; project meeting,
9 October 2026.*

## What it delivered

- **Documentation.** The theoretical documentation of the model, checked against
  the code. It is the project's main output.
- **Energy technology model.** Tested and documented as part of the model.
- **Model structure.** Modules separated from the model set-up and named to
  match the documentation.
- **Energy markets.** A general treatment of energy margins in place of a
  hard-coded one.
- **User guide.** Not published at the launch of this website. It will be added
  later.
- **Publication.** The model code is published as open source in the
  [repository on GitHub](https://github.com/DREAM-DK/GREU). The documentation
  is a PDF on this website. The licence text is to be confirmed by DREAM.
- **Support to the teams.** Austria, Belgium, Finland and Italy had individual
  support until the project closed at the end of 2026.

*Source: project meeting, 9 October 2026 (the work in progress then); Chapter 1
§ Background.*
